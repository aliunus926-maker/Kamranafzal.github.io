Our Mountain Heritage

From the glacial-fed orchards of Skardu directly to your home across Pakistan.

Rooted in Purity Since 1999

Welcome to Karakorum Harvest — a family-owned dry fruit venture rooted in Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan. Our journey began in 1999 when our founder Ali Ghulam started sourcing and selling the finest dry fruits directly from Skardu's orchards and markets.

High altitude, glacier waters, and untainted Himalayan air produce some of the most nutrient-dense nuts and fruits in the world. For over two decades, our commitment has been absolute: zero chemicals, zero artificial syrups, and direct partnership with local growers.

Today, headquartered with logistical facilities in Rawalpindi, we guarantee safe, vacuum-sealed shipping to every corner of Pakistan through established courier networks.
