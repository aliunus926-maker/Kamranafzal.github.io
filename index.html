Our Story

Welcome to Karakorum Harvest — a family-owned dry fruit business rooted in Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan. Our journey began in 1999 when our founder Ali Ghulam started sourcing and selling the finest dry fruits directly from Skardu's orchards and markets.

Today, we bring premium, 100% natural dry fruits to customers across Pakistan — with no middlemen and no compromise on quality.
